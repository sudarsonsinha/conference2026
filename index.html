Prof. Elena Rostova

Institute of Sustainable Chemistry, Zurich

Keynote Speaker

Keynote Topic: Catalytic Transformation of Biomass

Prof. Rostova has led pioneering research in sustainable catalytic pathways for converting agricultural waste into industrial-grade bio-monomers. She holds over 20 global patents in green chemical synthesis.

Her lecture will detail high-efficiency catalytic frameworks designed to operate under mild conditions, significantly reducing carbon footprints in chemical manufacturing.

Dr. Aris Thorne

Department of Nanomaterials, MIT

Keynote Speaker

Keynote Topic: Nanoplasmonic Energy Harvesting

Dr. Thorne specializes in theoretical computational chemistry and plasmon-driven catalysis. His work bridges high-level Gaussian/ORCA quantum simulations with real-world solar light harvesting.

Prof. Sunita Rao

National Institute of Material Science

Invited Speaker

Keynote Topic: MOFs for Environmental Remediation

Prof. Rao's team focuses on designing porous metal-organic frameworks capable of selective industrial pollutant absorption and carbon sequestration.

Scope

Conference Tracks

Submissions are welcomed across major interdisciplinary domains.

Track 1: Green Chemistry & Catalysis

Atom-economic synthesis, bio-catalytic pathways, solvent-free transformations, and eco-friendly chemical processes.

Track 2: Nanomaterials & Energy

Nanoplasmonics, photovoltaic materials, battery storage technologies, and smart functional nanostructures.

Track 3: Computational Molecular Design

Quantum chemical modeling (ORCA/Gaussian), molecular dynamics, machine learning for material discovery.
